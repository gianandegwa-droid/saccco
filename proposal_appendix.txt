ABSTRACT
This research investigates the challenges associated with the management of membership records at MemberLink SACCO in Nyeri and explores a suitable digital solution for improving the accuracy, accessibility and reliability of member information. The current membership record-management approach relies on paper folders, SMS messages and spreadsheets, resulting in fragmented information and difficulties in maintaining consistent and up-to-date records. The problem has contributed to missing member details, duplicate records, delays in processing member-related services, increased employee workload and concerns among members regarding fairness and reliability. The study therefore aims to investigate the existing membership record-management challenges, explore digital software solutions that can address the identified challenges, and conduct a feasibility assessment to determine a suitable solution for implementation.
